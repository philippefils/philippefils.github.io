MIXED-SIGNAL HARDWARE

Multichannel SiPM Scintillation Detector

A cost-effective, modular scintillation detector designed for automated PET radiopharmaceutical synthesis.

PROJECT TYPE Engineering Capstone
APPLICATION Medical Instrumentation
HARDWARE SiPM · ESP32 · Analog Front-End
TOOLS Altium Designer · LTSpice

01 — OVERVIEW

Project Overview

In collaboration with the Montreal Neurological Institute, my team and I developed a multichannel scintillation detector for monitoring radioactivity during the synthesis of positron emission tomography (PET) radiopharmaceuticals.

The system uses silicon photomultipliers (SiPMs) to detect photon emissions from radioactive samples and convert them into electrical pulses. These signals are conditioned using analog amplification and peak detection before being sampled by a microcontroller and transmitted to a PC-based interface.

The goal was to develop a cost-effective, modular, and scalable alternative to commercial detection systems while maintaining the performance required for automated synthesis.

02 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

System Architecture

The detector was designed as a modular signal-processing system, with each stage responsible for converting the SiPM output into usable digital data.

The signal path consists of the SiPM sensor, analog signal conditioning, peak detection, ADC acquisition, and microcontroller-based data transmission to the user interface.

03 — HARDWARE DESIGN

Mixed-Signal Hardware

A major focus of the project was designing the analog front-end required to condition the low-level SiPM signals. The circuit incorporates amplification and peak detection stages to produce a signal suitable for microcontroller acquisition.

The analog stages were designed and tuned to preserve the relevant characteristics of the detector pulses while providing sufficient signal amplitude for ADC sampling.

Particular attention was given to signal integrity, power distribution, and the interaction between the analog and digital portions of the system.

04 — PCB DESIGN

PCB Development

The system was implemented on a custom PCB designed in Altium Designer. The layout integrates the analog signal processing circuitry, power management, microcontroller, and supporting components into a compact hardware platform.

The PCB layout was developed with particular consideration for mixed-signal design, power integrity, grounding, component placement, and routing of sensitive analog signals.

05 — EMBEDDED SYSTEM

Data Acquisition & Firmware

An ESP32 microcontroller was used to sample the conditioned detector signals and transmit the acquired data to a computer for visualization and analysis.

ADC Acquisition

Configured analog-to-digital acquisition to capture the processed detector signal.

Embedded Firmware

Developed firmware in C++ to control acquisition and communicate measurement data.

Data Transmission

Transmitted measurement data to a PC-based interface for real-time monitoring.

System Integration

Integrated sensing, analog processing, firmware, power, and software into a complete system.

06 — TESTING & VALIDATION

Testing & Validation

The hardware was tested throughout development to verify circuit functionality and system performance. Individual circuit stages were evaluated before integrating them into the complete detector.

Testing included electrical measurements, signal characterization, and validation of the interaction between the analog, digital, and embedded portions of the system.

Engineering Challenges

System-Level Integration

Combined sensing, signal processing, embedded acquisition, and software into one functional system.
