Formula Electric Shutdown Loop Board

Diagnostic PCB designed to monitor the shutdown loop of a Formula Electric vehicle and simplify electrical fault identification.

Digital Logic PCB Design MOSFET Automotive

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Diagnostic monitoring for a vehicle shutdown loop

As part of McGill Formula Electric, I designed a PCB to monitor the state of the vehicle's shutdown loop and make electrical faults significantly easier to identify.

The Formula Electric vehicle relies on a shutdown loop containing 13 critical systems. Each system must provide a valid signal for the vehicle to remain powered. If one of these systems fails, its signal causes the shutdown loop to open, preventing the vehicle from powering on or shutting it down if it is already running.

The diagnostic board provides a visual indication of which system has caused the shutdown condition, reducing the time required to locate a fault within the vehicle's electrical system.

SYSTEM OPERATION

13-channel shutdown monitoring

Each of the 13 monitored systems provides a signal representing its operational state. Under normal conditions, the corresponding diagnostic LED remains off.

When a monitored system fails, its signal is driven low. This activates the corresponding PMOS transistor and illuminates its LED, providing an immediate visual indication of which shutdown loop input has entered a fault state.

Design objective

The primary design objective was to turn an otherwise difficult electrical troubleshooting process into a simple visual diagnosis. Instead of tracing the shutdown loop manually, the board identifies the affected system through its dedicated fault indicator.

CIRCUIT DESIGN

MOSFET-based fault indication

The diagnostic channels use PMOS transistors to interface the shutdown loop signals with the LED indicators. The circuit is arranged so that a low fault signal activates the corresponding indicator, allowing each shutdown-loop condition to be represented independently.

The repeated channel architecture simplifies the design by using the same logic structure for each of the 13 monitored systems. This provides a straightforward relationship between each vehicle subsystem and its corresponding diagnostic indicator.

PCB DESIGN

From electrical design to PCB layout

I designed the PCB using Altium Designer, translating the shutdown-loop monitoring circuitry into a compact board intended for integration into the vehicle's electrical system.

The board layout organizes the 13 diagnostic channels and their corresponding indicators while providing the necessary electrical interfaces for connection to the vehicle shutdown loop.

ENGINEERING IMPACT

Simplifying vehicle troubleshooting

The shutdown loop is safety-critical to the operation of the Formula Electric vehicle, but identifying which subsystem caused a shutdown can otherwise require significant troubleshooting.

By providing an individual LED for each monitored system, the diagnostic board reduces the fault-isolation process to a direct visual indication. This makes troubleshooting faster and helps the team identify the subsystem requiring investigation before performing more detailed electrical diagnostics.
